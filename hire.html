Frequently asked

Quick answers to the things most clients ask before booking.

How is payment handled?
50% upfront after you approve the scoped proposal, 50% on delivery. Indian clients pay via Razorpay (UPI / cards / netbanking). International clients can pay via Stripe or Wise — ask me for a link.
What if the model doesn't hit the accuracy I want?
I'll always give a realistic baseline estimate in the proposal — not a promise of a specific number. If the deliverable doesn't meet the scoped acceptance criteria, I keep iterating at no extra cost until it does, or refund the second installment.
Do I get the source code?
Yes — full source, trained model files, and documentation. Delivered via a private GitHub repo or zip, your choice. The code is yours once the final payment clears.
Will you sign an NDA?
Happily. Send me your standard NDA along with the brief, or I can send you a simple one-page mutual NDA to sign before we discuss specifics.
What if my project doesn't fit any of the packages?
Pick "Custom / Not sure yet" in the form and describe what you need. I'll scope and quote it like the standard tiers — same fixed-price approach.
